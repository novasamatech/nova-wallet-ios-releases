
Bittensor (TAO) staking is here in Nova Wallet v11.0.1!

- Stake TAO on the root network or buy into any subnet.
- Compare subnets by price, APY, and pool size, then pick the one you like.
- Track your earnings, average buy price, and positions in the new "Your Bittensor" portfolio.
- Added XCM transfers and swap support for Snowbridge assets.
- Various improvements and stability fixes.

Hotfixes:
- Fixed an issue where selling from all validators in TAO staking could cause the entire swap to fail if any validator held only a dust stake.
