
Asset Management improvements are here in Nova Wallet v10.10.1.

- Improved asset management: your asset list, by default, now only shows the tokens that matter to you, such as the ones you own - additionally, any supported token can still be added in a few taps.
- Fixed an issue where Hydration swaps with a specified receive amount could fail with a slippage error.
- Fixed an issue where some Hydration swaps showed an unusually high price impact.
- Fixed an issue where Hydration governance unlocking periods could be shown incorrectly.
- Various improvements and stability fixes.
